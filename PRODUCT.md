## Writing voice
Andrew explicitly requested his own voice, grounded in his original conversational draft: first-person, curious, skeptical, concrete, conversational, and comfortable with uncertainty and occasional profanity. Preserve phrases and examples he actually supplied, including “a new line item from the clouds,” “default toggled off/on,” the surprising social-media-intern example, and the Excel comparison. Keep the connective reasoning; do not turn everything into short punchlines, a tidy consulting framework, slogans, or generic AI prose. Do not invent personal experiences or credentials. All three ideas belong together: the unexamined default flipped, the opportunity widened in unexpected places, and deeper investment still needs scrutiny.
